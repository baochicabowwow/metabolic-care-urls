Protein budget

A running balance, not a bedtime diary.

Set the range your metabolic dietitian gave you, per kilogram or per day. Krestrel works a per-kg range out against your child’s latest weight and adds up meals and formula as you go.

  • Range shown as a calm band, never a red or green verdict
  • Amino-acid estimates from your dietitian’s factor
  • Past days never rewrite themselves

Meal logging

Weigh it, tap it, see what’s left.

Search one result per food with a raw or cooked picker, enter grams from the scale or a package label, and save the meals you make every week.

  • “g left today” right on the meal log
  • Food data from USDA, Canada, the UK, Germany and France
  • Bulk-import your dietitian’s food list from CSV

Formula & feeds

Formula counts toward the day, automatically.

Log bottles of breast milk, formula or metabolic formula. Krestrel works out the protein from your child’s formula concentration, and a change of recipe applies from the date you choose.

  • Shared timers for nursing, sleep and tummy time
  • Yesterday’s feed at this time, one tap away
  • Move the start of the day for overnight feeds

Labs

Every blood level, against your clinic’s target.

Import a lab PDF or enter values by hand. Each result sits against the target band your clinic set, and the trend chart shades that band so you can see the pattern before the appointment.

  • Nothing saves until you review it
  • “Less than” values and one-sided ranges supported
  • Original reports kept privately with the results

Growth

The weight that sets the protein range.

Weight, length and head circumference on WHO percentile curves. A new weight updates a per-kg protein range from the next day.

Frequently asked questions

What is Krestrel?

Krestrel is a free iPhone and iPad app for parents and caregivers of children with inherited metabolic conditions. It keeps the whole care log in one place: meals and daily protein, formula and feeds, lab results, medicines, growth, appointments, a health log and an emergency plan, shared with everyone who helps care for your child.

Which metabolic conditions is Krestrel for?

Krestrel is built for families managing protein-restricted metabolic diets, including PKU, MSUD, homocystinuria (HCU), glutaric aciduria type 1 (GA1), isovaleric acidemia (IVA) and pyridoxine-dependent epilepsy (PDE). It tracks total protein against the range your metabolic dietitian sets and can show amino-acid estimates such as phenylalanine, leucine or lysine using the factor your dietitian gives you.

Does Krestrel tell me how much protein my child should have?

No. Krestrel is a record-keeping tool, not medical advice. Your metabolic dietitian sets the range; Krestrel does the arithmetic and shows where today’s total sits. It never suggests targets, diets or doses.

Is Krestrel free?

Yes. Krestrel is free to download and use, with no ads and no third-party tracking.

Can grandparents, sitters or a co-parent use it too?

Yes. Invite caregivers into your care circle by email or link. Primary caregivers choose what each person can see and do, such as logging entries, seeing lab results or editing targets.

Does Krestrel work with ChatGPT and Claude?

Yes. You can connect Krestrel to Claude or ChatGPT and ask about, or add to, your child’s care log in plain words. You choose each child and each area, and whether the assistant can only read or also add. Lab values an assistant reads from a report wait as drafts until you confirm them in the app.

Is my child’s health information private?

Your data is not for sale. Krestrel has no advertising, no third-party analytics and no cross-app tracking, and you can delete your account and your child’s records at any time from Settings. See the privacy policy for details.

Is there an Android version?

Krestrel is available for iPhone and iPad today.
