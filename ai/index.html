What it can do

Look up and log, never change the plan.

Look up (Read)

  • Care summary: day-by-day feeds, protein, sleep, diapers, doses, health events and growth
  • Diet: protein against the range your care team set, and the meals logged
  • Food search: foods in your list with protein per 100 g and servings
  • Labs: results with the target range and whether each was in range
  • Medicines, appointments, open questions and the emergency plan

Add (Read & add)

  • Log a meal from foods it matched with confidence; unsure matches are asked about, not guessed
  • Log a feed, a medicine dose or a health-log entry
  • Add a provider, an appointment or a question for the next visit
  • Propose lab results read from a report, saved as a draft you confirm in the app
  • No editing, no deleting, no settings, no photos

You stay in control

Per child, per area, read or add.

When you connect, you pick each child and each area (feeds, diet, labs, medicines, appointments, emergency plan) and whether the assistant may only read or also add. Change it any time in Settings → AI & connected apps.

  • Recent AI activity lists every request
  • One switch turns AI apps off for the family
  • Helpers need a primary caregiver’s OK
  • Disconnect takes effect immediately

Frequently asked questions

Can Claude or ChatGPT change my child’s protein range or medicine doses?

No. The Krestrel connection has no tool that changes a protein range, formula, medicine or dose, and it can’t edit or delete anything. Assistants are told to send those questions to your care team.

What can an AI assistant add to Krestrel?

With Read & add access it can log meals, bottle feeds, medicine doses and health-log entries, add a provider, an appointment or a question for the next visit, and propose lab results. Proposed lab results are saved as a draft until you confirm them in the app.

Who can connect an AI app?

A primary caregiver can connect one. Other caregivers can only connect if a primary caregiver turns on Connect AI apps for them, and a primary caregiver can turn AI apps off for the whole family.

Do I need a paid Claude or ChatGPT plan?

You need a Claude or ChatGPT plan that lets you add a custom connector. Those options change often, so check the current plan details in the AI app.

Is this medical advice?

No. Krestrel shows the records your family entered. Talk to your metabolic care team about anything to do with your child’s diet or treatment.

Claude is a product of Anthropic and ChatGPT is a product of OpenAI; Krestrel is not affiliated with either. Once information reaches an AI app, that company’s privacy terms cover it. Krestrel is a record-keeping tool, not medical advice.
