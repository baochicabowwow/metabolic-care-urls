How to Ask Claude or ChatGPT About Your Child’s Care Log, Safely

By the Krestrel team · Published 28 November 2026 · 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • You choose each child, each area, and No access, Read, or Read & add.
  • An assistant can log meals, feeds, doses and health events, but can’t edit, delete, or change ranges or doses.
  • Lab values read from a report are drafts until you confirm them in the app.
  • Every request is listed in Recent AI activity, and one switch turns AI apps off for the family.

Krestrel can connect to Claude and ChatGPT so you can ask about, or add to, your child’s care log in plain words. It’s useful at 3am, in the car park before clinic, or with a toddler on one hip. It is also your child’s health information, so here is exactly what the connection does and how to keep it tight.

What can an AI assistant see in Krestrel?

Only what you choose when you connect. For each child you pick each area (feeds, diet, labs, medicines, appointments, emergency plan, health log) and set it to No access, Read, or Read & add. Untick a child to leave them out entirely. A helper in your care circle can never give an AI app more than they can see themselves.

What can it add, and what can’t it touch?

Which prompts work best?

  • Diet: “What has Maya eaten today, and how much protein is that so far?”
  • Logging: “Log 120 ml of formula for Maya at 7:30 this morning.”
  • Trends: “How did Maya sleep over the last three nights?”
  • Appointments: “When is our next clinic appointment? Add a question about her evening formula.”
  • Labs: “Show Maya’s last four leucine results against her target range.”
  • Emergency: “What does Maya’s emergency plan say to do if she’s vomiting?”

Naming Krestrel (“Using Krestrel, …”) helps if the assistant doesn’t pick it up. For meals, see how I log meals with Claude.

Why do lab results wait as drafts?

An AI can misread a number or a unit, and lab values matter. When an assistant reads values from a PDF or photo of a report, Krestrel saves them as a draft under To review. You check each value against the report, switch off anything wrong, and save. Only what you save goes into the lab history.

How do I keep it private?

  • Give the least access that does the job. Read on labs and diet is enough to ask questions.
  • Only connect from your own account, and start each step yourself. Don’t approve a Krestrel sign-in link someone sends you.
  • Check Recent AI activity now and then, in Settings → AI & connected apps.
  • Remember whose terms apply. Once information reaches Claude or ChatGPT, Anthropic’s or OpenAI’s privacy terms cover it, not Krestrel’s.
  • Turn it off for the whole family with one switch, or disconnect a single app.

How do I connect?

Follow the Claude guide or the ChatGPT guide. Both take about three minutes. The Works with AI page lists everything the connection can do.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to connect my child’s health records to Claude or ChatGPT?

You decide what the assistant can see: each child, each area, read or add. It can’t edit, delete or change settings, lab values it reads wait as drafts, and every request is listed in Recent AI activity. Once information reaches the AI app, that company’s privacy terms cover it, so share only what you need.

Can a grandparent or sitter connect their own AI app?

Only if a primary caregiver turns on Connect AI apps for them. A primary caregiver can also turn AI apps off for the whole family.

How do I disconnect?

In Krestrel, open Settings → AI & connected apps and tap Disconnect. It takes effect immediately.

Will the assistant give medical advice about the numbers?

Krestrel tells assistants that the values are your family’s records, not medical advice, and to send changes to your care team. Treat anything an assistant says about treatment as a question for your team.
