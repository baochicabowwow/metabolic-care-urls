Connect ChatGPT to Krestrel

Step by step. It takes about three minutes. Last updated 28 November 2026.

Only connect from your own ChatGPT account. Start each step yourself. If someone sends you a Krestrel sign-in link, don't approve it, even if it says ChatGPT. Approving it would connect their AI account to your child's records.
ChatGPT renames its menus often. OpenAI has called these "connectors", "apps" and "plugins" at different times. If a label below doesn't match what you see, look for Developer mode and a way to add or create a custom connector. Not every ChatGPT plan offers developer mode.

Before you start

  • You need a Krestrel account and the Krestrel app on your phone.
  • AI apps must be turned on for your family. If you're not the primary caregiver, a primary caregiver has to allow you first. In the Krestrel app, they open Settings, tap your name under Circle, and turn on Connect AI apps.
  • You need a ChatGPT account whose plan allows developer mode and custom connectors. On a workspace plan, an admin may need to allow it first.

1. Copy the connector address in Krestrel

In the Krestrel app, open Settings → AI & connected apps. Under How to connect, tap Copy next to the connector address.

2. Turn on developer mode in ChatGPT

Use ChatGPT on the web (chatgpt.com). Open Settings, then the section for apps or connectors, then Advanced settings, and turn on Developer mode.

3. Create the Krestrel connector

In the same section, choose Create (or Add) to make a new connector:

  • Name: Krestrel
  • MCP server URL: paste the address you copied in step 1
  • Authentication: OAuth. Leave any client ID and secret empty; ChatGPT registers itself with Krestrel.

Confirm that you trust the connector and create it. A Krestrel sign-in page opens. If it doesn't, open the Krestrel connector and choose Connect.

4. Sign in with your Krestrel account

Enter the email and password you use in the Krestrel app, not your ChatGPT login. You can also ask for a sign-in code by email. The page is on krestrel.com; check the address bar before you type your password.

5. Check it's ChatGPT, choose what it may see, then Allow

First, check the top of the page. It should say Connect ChatGPT to Krestrel? with Recognised: ChatGPT, and it should say it will return to chatgpt.com. If it names anything else, or you didn't just start this yourself, choose Don't allow.

Then, for each child, choose what ChatGPT may do in each area: No access, Read, or Read & add. Untick a child to keep that child out completely. Pick how long the connection lasts (30 days unless you change it), read the note under Before you allow, then choose Allow. The consent page looks the same as in the Claude guide, with ChatGPT's name instead.

Once information reaches ChatGPT, OpenAI's privacy terms cover it, not Krestrel's.

6. Check the connection in Krestrel

Back in Settings → AI & connected apps, ChatGPT now appears with what it may see and when access ends. If it isn't there yet, pull down to refresh.

Using Krestrel in ChatGPT

In a new chat, turn Krestrel on from the tools or connectors menu under the message box if it isn't already, then ask in plain words:

  • "Using Krestrel, what has Maya eaten today, and how much protein is that so far?"
  • "Log a 120 ml bottle for Maya at 7:30 this morning."
  • "When is Maya's next appointment? Add a question for it about sleep."
  • "Here's Maya's lab report. Add the results to Krestrel." (They're saved as a draft you confirm in the app.)

ChatGPT may ask you to confirm before it adds anything. See everything the connection can and can't do, and how one parent logs meals this way.

If something goes wrong

I can't find developer mode or a way to add a connector

Your ChatGPT plan may not include it, or your workspace admin may manage it. ChatGPT also moves these settings from time to time; search ChatGPT's help for "developer mode".

The sign-in page says "Krestrel can't connect to this app"

Krestrel only connects to ChatGPT, Claude and AI apps running on your own computer. Go back, and don't sign in from that link again. Start again from step 3 in your own ChatGPT account.

The page says "AI apps are turned off" or "Ask your primary caregiver first"

A primary caregiver needs to turn AI apps on for the family in Settings → AI & connected apps, or allow you under Circle.

ChatGPT can't see a child or an area

That child or area is set to No access, or you only gave Read and asked it to add something. Change it in Krestrel under Settings → AI & connected apps → Change access, then start a new chat.

How do I disconnect?

In Krestrel, use Settings → AI & connected apps → Disconnect. That takes effect right away. You can also delete the connector in ChatGPT's settings. Read more in the Privacy Policy.
